## A note on the reporting figures

`bd-reporting-citywise.svg` shows a lead trend and per-city map-pack percentages
for Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet and Khulna. **These are illustrative, not client
data**, and the diagram says so on its face — the footer reads *"Illustrative
reporting view"*. Keep that caption if the numbers are edited, or replace the
values with real reporting and remove it. Presenting invented figures as results
on a marketing page is the thing the caption exists to prevent.
